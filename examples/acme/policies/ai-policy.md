# Acme Lending AI Policy

Version 2.1. Approved by the Executive Committee on 14 March 2026. Policy owner: Chief Risk Officer. Next review: March 2027.

## 1. Scope
This AI policy applies to every AI system that Acme builds, buys or embeds in a product, including vendor tools.

## 2. Principles
We use AI in ways that are fair, transparent, safe and accountable. Human oversight is required for any decision that affects a customer's access to credit.

## 3. Prohibited use
Staff must not use AI for social scoring, emotion recognition of employees, or any purpose not permitted by law. Our risk appetite for customer harm from AI is low.

## 4. Roles and responsibilities
The AI Governance Committee, chaired by the Chief Risk Officer as accountable executive, approves high-risk use cases. Every AI system has a named system owner. Issues are escalated to the Committee within five working days. Staff can raise concerns through the Speak Up line.

## 5. Risk classification
Each use case receives a risk tier (prohibited, high-risk, limited, minimal) before development starts, using the classification criteria in Annex A, which follow Annex III of the EU AI Act. The classification rationale is recorded in the AI system inventory.

## 6. Generative AI
Staff may only use approved tools for generative AI. Do not paste confidential or customer data into public tools. A guardrail and hallucination evaluation standard will be developed in 2027.
